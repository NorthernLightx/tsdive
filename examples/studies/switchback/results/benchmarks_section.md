## REAL: switchback schedules (3W, TEP, Turbine Upgrade, SKAB)

The `randomization` arm is scored beside `block_t`, `hac`, `ewc` and `naive` intervals on the same schedules and beside the first-half against second-half split of the shift interval study. The rows read the confirmatory run on fresh assignments; the first registration and its verdict are in [examples/studies/switchback/REPORT.md](examples/studies/switchback/REPORT.md), with the method and refusals.
